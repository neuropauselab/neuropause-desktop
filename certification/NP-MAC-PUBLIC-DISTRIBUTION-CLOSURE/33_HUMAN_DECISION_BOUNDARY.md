# Human decision boundary

Computer-B may measure, verify, package, test, report and block. It did all of these. It did NOT: publish, push, tag, notarize, designate a baseline or verifier, adopt a claim, assign a regulatory class, or authorize a release.

Open human decisions: RELEASE_VERSION · MAC_ARCHITECTURE · push branch · Apple notarization credential path · Windows certificate · domain/hosting · dependency findings · GATE-INTENDED-USE · regulatory review · VERIFIER_DESIGNATION · BASELINE_AUTHORITY · PILOT_CLAIM_SET_ADOPTION · HD-19-CLASS · PUBLIC_RELEASE_AUTHORIZATION.

A PUBLIC_RELEASE_AUTHORIZATION must carry: RELEASE_ID, ARTIFACT_DIGEST(s), SOURCE_COMMIT, VERSION, PLATFORMS, INTENDED_USE, REGULATORY_STATUS, SECURITY_STATUS, AI_STATUS, USER_PILOT_STATUS, ROLLBACK_STATUS, AUTHORITY_BASIS, HUMAN_ACTOR, TIMESTAMP, DECISION. Template: certification/public-launch/PUBLIC_RELEASE_AUTHORIZATION.template.json (unsigned, unfilled).
