# GETTING-STARTED — first-run guide (customer-facing)

The user-facing quickstart the product lacked (audit finding C6).

## 1. Download & install
- **macOS**: open the `.dmg` → drag NeuroPause to Applications → right-click →
  Open → Open (once, unsigned RC).
- **Windows**: run `NeuroPause Setup.exe` → SmartScreen → More info → Run anyway
  (once) → installs per-user.

## 2. Create your account
On first launch the onboarding wizard greets you. Sign up with email (no OAuth
provider required) — you're connecting to the live NeuroPause backend.

## 3. Set up your organization
Name your organization; you become its owner. Invite teammates later from the
Organization view.

## 4. Connect your first tool
Open Connectors → Google Calendar connects immediately and starts populating your
Timeline. GitHub, Notion, and Slack are available once configured.

## 5. Turn on the AI (optional but recommended)
Install [Ollama](https://ollama.com) and run `ollama serve` for fully on-device
AI. The Diagnostics panel shows the engine's status and tells you exactly what to
do if it isn't running.

## 6. See the value
- **Timeline** fills with your connected activity.
- **Mission Brief** (Enterprise → Briefings) generates your daily brief.
- **Founder AI** (AI Workforce) answers questions about your own work.

## 7. Share feedback
Welcome → Share feedback sends notes; the Export button hands you a JSON of
everything for support.

## Support
Operations → support bundle exports diagnostics. During early access, feedback
and support run through the in-app tools.
