Questions

Good to know

Is NeuroPause just another AI chatbot?

No. It's an operating layer that sits above your existing AI tools and SaaS apps. It connects them, remembers your work across them, and gives you a brief and a searchable memory — the plumbing between your tools, not a replacement for them.

What can it actually see?

Only what you connect and authorize. Every connector uses official OAuth — your credentials are encrypted in your OS keychain — and activity tracking is permission-based. Your memory timeline is stored locally on your own machine.

Do I need to send my data to the cloud?

No. NeuroPause can run its AI fully on-device using Ollama, and your memory stays local. You choose what, if anything, leaves your machine.

Which tools does it connect to?

Sixteen, including GitHub, Notion, Slack, and Google Calendar with live sync today, and OAuth-ready support for Claude, Gemini, Figma, Jira, Linear, Google Drive, Microsoft 365 and more.

Why does the app show a warning on first launch?

The current release candidate isn't code-signed yet, so macOS and Windows ask you to confirm once. It's the standard unsigned-app prompt — signing is on the roadmap for general availability.

Is it really free?

Yes — the Free tier runs the full desktop app. Paid plans add all connectors, unlimited memory, the daily brief, and team features. During early access, everyone's on the free tier.
