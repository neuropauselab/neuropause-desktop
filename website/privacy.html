NeuroPause Lab Pvt Ltd ("NeuroPause", "we", "us") operates the NeuroPause desktop application and backend services. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address — used for authentication, account recovery, and support communication.
  • Display name and avatar — provided by you or your OAuth provider, shown in the application UI.
  • OAuth identities — when you sign in with Google, GitHub, Microsoft, or Apple, we store the provider identity to authenticate you. We do not store your OAuth provider password.

1.2 Device Information

  • Device identifier — a cryptographic identity established during device registration.
  • Platform, OS, architecture, app version — used for device management and compatibility.

1.3 Application Data

  • Organization membership — your role in organizations you belong to.
  • Synced data — documents and records you choose to sync through NeuroPause connectors.
  • Assistant conversations — questions you ask the AI assistant and its responses, stored locally on your device and (when synced) on our servers.

1.4 AI / AssistantAsk Processing

  • When you use the AI assistant, your query text is sent to the backend AI gateway.
  • The backend routes your query to a configured AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, or a self-hosted model).
  • We do not train AI models on your data. Your queries are processed for generating responses only.
  • Third-party AI provider terms apply to the processing performed by those providers.

1.5 Technical and Security Data

  • Audit log — security-relevant events (login, logout, permission changes) are logged with timestamps and IP addresses.
  • Error and performance telemetry — when the application encounters errors, diagnostic information may be logged on the server. No user content is included in error reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the NeuroPause application.
  • To authenticate you and maintain your sessions.
  • To process AI assistant queries on your behalf.
  • To enforce security controls (rate limiting, device trust, audit).
  • To communicate with you about your account (verification, password reset, support).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

  • OAuth providers (Google, GitHub, Microsoft, Apple) — for authentication.
  • AI model providers (as configured by the operator) — for processing assistant queries.
  • Cloudflare — CDN and edge security for the backend and download infrastructure.
  • DigitalOcean — cloud hosting for the backend infrastructure.
  • Razorpay — payment processing for paid plans (when billing is enabled).

4. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained while your account is active.
  • Sessions — refresh tokens expire after 30 days; access tokens expire after 15 minutes.
  • Audit logs — retained for security and compliance purposes.
  • Deleted accounts — upon confirmed account deletion, personal data is anonymized. Anonymized audit records may be retained for up to 30 days for legal compliance.

5. Data Export

You can export all data associated with your account at any time using the GET /auth/export API endpoint while authenticated. The export includes your profile, identities, sessions, organization memberships, devices, audit log, and sync state.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account using the POST /auth/delete-account endpoint. Deletion requires confirmation via POST /auth/confirm-delete-account. Upon confirmed deletion:

  • All sessions and access tokens are revoked immediately.
  • Your devices, OAuth identities, and application tokens are deleted.
  • Your user record is anonymized (email, name, avatar removed).
  • Audit log entries are redacted of personal information.

7. Security

  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id.
  • Refresh tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes only.
  • Device identity uses Ed25519 proof-of-possession.
  • All API communication uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Rate limiting is enforced on authentication and sensitive endpoints.
  • CI/CD pipeline includes SAST, secret scanning, and DAST controls.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (via data export).
  • Rectify inaccurate personal data.
  • Delete your personal data (via account deletion).
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Data portability (via data export in JSON format).

9. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries or to exercise your rights:

  • Security: security@neuropause033.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the application or via email. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
