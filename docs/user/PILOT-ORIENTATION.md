# Pilot Orientation

**One page · v1.0.0-rc.14 lineage · Phase 8.**

## What this pilot is

You are running a local-first enterprise workspace: 104 certified business modules across 13 families, plus AI workspace surfaces, on YOUR machine. Your data lives in local, backed-up, versioned stores — nothing leaves the device except what you explicitly connect (OAuth connectors, optional backend sync). There is no usage telemetry; crash reporting is opt-in and redacted.

## What we ask of you

Work real scenarios in your family (Finance, HR, Inventory/Procurement guides are bundled — Getting Started → Documentation). When something surprises you — a dead end, a confusing screen, a number you don't trust — capture it in **Getting Started → Feedback** the moment it happens. Feedback stays local: export it (one click) and send the file to your pilot contact weekly, or on request.

## When something breaks

Operations → Release Diagnostics → **Generate bundle** — it collects redacted logs, crash records, build identity and module state, then reveals the file in Finder. Send that with a sentence about what you were doing. If the app won't start twice in a row, it will offer safe mode; your data is covered by scheduled backups (Operations → Recovery Center) that include every business record.

## Honesty notes

Sections labeled **Preview** are real code, honestly labeled as not-yet-production. The build you have may be unsigned (see Installation for the Gatekeeper steps). The EULA and privacy notice bundled with this build are review drafts unless your pilot contact says otherwise.
